# Rerun log: Peddy H2O reference (LOWER/UPPER, 2025 + 2026) and EddyPro (5 min, biomet)

Started 2026-10-01. Plan: `~/.claude/plans/joyful-weaving-pixel.md`. Background: `CLAUDE_rerun_2025_LOWER_UPPER.md`.

## Why
- 2025 (processed elsewhere): UPPER H2O corrected with RH as 0–1 fraction (reference 100× too small); LOWER never corrected (no 2025 slow data).
- 2026: LOWER 16 m and UPPER 26 m TA/RH probes dead since early March 2026; logger code -7999 was not masked,
  so the reference was NaN/garbage and processed H2O for LOWER/UPPER was ~100 % NaN from March on.
- EddyPro templates pointed at the stale copy `/mnt/data1/PEA` (monthly runs failed since July 2026), used
  3-min averaging without biomet, and never overrode the template end times (last day of each month truncated).

## What changed (code)
- `DataProcessingScriptsJulia/src/read_data,jl`: `clean_tower_slowdata` masks -7999 and logger zeros, auto-detects
  RH fraction vs percent, bounds TA/RH/AP; new `build_reference_chain`, `interpolate_short_gaps!`, `H2O_REF_CHAIN`.
- `src/process_sensor.jl`: `ref_data` kwarg; reference on a regular 1-min grid spanning the day's fast data (NaN where
  missing, so no far-away reference); fast data filtered to the calendar day (raw daily files overlap ~25 h);
  H2O outside 0–400 mmol/m³ → NaN; per-day `{S}_h2o_ref_{YYYYMM}.csv`; processing log appends in resume mode.
- `scripts/run_sensor.jl` (new driver; `run_LOWER.jl`/`run_UPPER.jl` are wrappers). Env: `PEA_INPUT_BASE`,
  `PEA_HF_OUT`, `PEA_LF_OUT`, `PEA_YEAR`, `PEA_REF_CHAIN_<S>`. Flags: `--restart --from --to --slow-only --no-save-slow`.
- `make_biomet.py`: `--base`, logger codes/bounds, RH auto-scale, valid-share report.
- EddyPro: templates fixed to `/data/disk1/shared/PEA`, `avrg_len=5`; new engine `run_eddypro_months.sh`
  (per-job work dirs, `data_path`/`proj_file`/times/`project_id=PEA_<S>_<M>_5min_biomet`, project-specific
  fluxnet lookup, `ARCHIVE_DIR`); `run_bulk_eddypro.sh` and `run_monthly_eddypro.sh` are thin wrappers.
- Helpers: `pull_2025_from_jed.sh`, `rerun_peddy_h2o.sh <year>`, `check_h2o.py` (summary|days|compare).
